A small fix to RL Atlas 1.0.0.

**Download `rl_atlas.html` below and open it in your browser.** Nothing to install, no server, no account.

## Fixed

- A formula a little too wide for its column now shrinks until it fits. It used to stop a few pixels short, so its end
  was cut off and some browsers showed a scrollbar under it: the search rule in the MCTS story, the formulas of the
  imitation, prioritized sweeping and MC vs TD stories, and two formulas each in *Value error* and the *policy gradient
  theorem*.
- A story's formula fits its card while it is still faint, before a step lights it up.

Everything else is as in [1.0.0](https://github.com/DrippingSoup22/rl_atlas/releases/tag/v1.0.0).
