The first release of **RL Atlas**: a visual, interactive guide to reinforcement learning, from the agent–environment
loop to PPO and SAC, in a single HTML file that works offline.

**Download `rl_atlas.html` below and open it in your browser.** Nothing to install, no server, no account.

## What's inside

- **95 stations in 14 parts**, all written: the core of Sutton & Barto's *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*
  (bandits, dynamic programming, Monte Carlo, temporal difference, n-step methods and traces, planning, function
  approximation, policy gradients), then deep RL (DQN and its extensions, A2C, TRPO, PPO, DDPG, TD3, SAC), the
  practical toolbox, and where the field goes next (MCTS and AlphaZero, model-based deep RL, offline RL, imitation,
  multi-agent RL, RLHF).
- **Every algorithm three ways**: a **story** (scroll the text, and the picture follows it step by step; 79 of them),
  a **textbook** chapter (definitions, derivations, theorems and figures recomputed live), and a one-page **card**
  (formula, pseudocode, perks, flaws, knobs and quiz questions).
- **The Lab**, with 58 experiments: watch algorithms learn side by side, one pseudocode line at a time or 50 episodes
  a second, and see how often their settings end well over 20 seeds, and how those odds move with each knob. Move an
  experiment to another world (Cliff Walking, Frozen Lake, Taxi, the windy gridworld, CartPole, Acrobot and more) with
  **Change world**.
- **Deep RL in the browser**: DQN and its extensions, A2C, TRPO and PPO on CartPole, and PPO, DDPG, TD3 and SAC on
  Pendulum, recorded over 20 seeds by the guide's own JavaScript trainer; any other seed or setting trains on the spot.
- A map with three views (the metro line in reading order, a family tree per line, and Sutton & Barto's unified view),
  search across the whole atlas (`Ctrl K`), a symbols page, and a dark theme.

## Good to know

- Developed and tested with Chromium, the engine of Chrome, Edge and Brave. Firefox and Safari should work, but have not
  been checked yet. The layout adapts to phone screens (checked in an emulated phone, not yet on a real one).
- Your progress (the stations you have read) stays in your browser.
- The file is about 8 MB: everything, fonts and recorded runs included, is inside it.
