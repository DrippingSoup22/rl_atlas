Fixes from the second check of RL Atlas in real browsers (Chrome and Brave on a Windows laptop), which also confirmed
the fixes of 1.0.2.

**Download `rl_atlas.html` below and open it in your browser.** Nothing to install, no server, no account.

## Fixed

- **No freeze when a run trained in the page arrives.** In a deep lab, the moment a newly trained run was drawn
  froze the page for 76 to 130 ms. Its film strip's small maps of what the network thinks now take a tenth of the
  drawing (and lose the faint grid they showed), so the page stays free. The deep labs also open a little faster.
- **No freeze when Play starts a lab again from the beginning at a fast rate.** The first rounds take the longest to
  work out. A lab now works out the round Play shows first while it waits, as it does while playing. Before, the PPO
  maze froze for 50 to 120 ms each time it was played again at 20 rounds a second.
- **No freeze when a lab switches to its typical seed.** Some seconds after a lab opens, once its 20 seeds have run,
  it plays the typical one. Working out that run froze the page for up to about 120 ms (A2C maze, PPO maze), just
  when a reader might press Play. It is now worked out in short slices, while the run on screen stays.

Everything else is as in [1.0.2](https://github.com/DrippingSoup22/rl_atlas/releases/tag/v1.0.2).
