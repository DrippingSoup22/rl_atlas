Fixes from the first check of RL Atlas in real browsers (Chrome, Brave and Edge on a Windows laptop).

**Download `rl_atlas.html` below and open it in your browser.** Nothing to install, no server, no account.

## Fixed

- **Hover cards stay inside the window.** A long card, such as TRPO's from the PPO card's table, could open above the
  window's top edge. A card now opens below its term or above it, wherever it fits, and otherwise stays inside the
  window. A long card is also wider, so it is shorter.
- **Story formulas fit narrow windows.** At about 1024 pixels wide, the formulas of the MCTS, A2C, bias vs variance and
  prioritized sweeping stories were still cut off, with a scrollbar under them. When shrinking is not enough, a
  formula's two parts now stack on two lines.
- **Stories prepare their runs while you scroll.** The PPO story could say "Working out this run…" for 2 to 3
  seconds on a slow scroll; the page now goes on preparing runs, a little each frame, while you scroll.
- **Labs played at a few rounds a second no longer freeze between rounds.** The next round is now worked out ahead,
  a few milliseconds per frame. Before, the PPO maze froze for up to 180 ms at each round, and the A2C maze for 50 to
  90 ms.
- **The header on a phone** keeps the page's title and shortens the part's name with "…", instead of cutting it off
  mid-word.
- **The symbols page fits a phone screen.**
- **Smaller things:** in a deep lab, the title stays at the top beside its tall card of settings. A lab's seed button
  reads "▸ Typical: seed N" when the typical run is ready to play. The Welcome page says where your progress is kept
  (in your browser, so clearing its site data forgets it), and no longer mentions highlighting that is gone.

Everything else is as in [1.0.1](https://github.com/DrippingSoup22/rl_atlas/releases/tag/v1.0.1).
