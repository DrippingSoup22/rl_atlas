RL Atlas 1.2.0 adds an appendix on the mathematics behind the formulas, a cleaner map, and a layout pass over the
whole guide: formulas sized to the room they have, no line that starts with a comma or a sign, and story pictures
that stay whole on screen.

**Download `rl_atlas.html` below and open it in your browser.** Nothing to install, no server, no account.

## New: the appendix

27 short pages on the mathematics the guide leans on, none of it about reinforcement learning: sums and products,
probability and conditional probability, expectations, variance, Markov chains, norms, vectors and matrices, fixed
points and contractions, limits, derivatives and gradients, the softmax and more, in six groups. The pages that use
them link to them, with hover cards and search results like any station; each page lists the stations that use it.
A small dotted **Σ Appendix** stop on the map opens it from every view.

## Also new

- **Parts 0 and 1, revised.** The Bellman story checks the equation cell by cell before using it as a recipe; the
  textbooks derive their equations step by step and explain the mathematics where it is used.
- **The map's unified view is a table of all 103 stations**: across, the families of methods; down, how far an update
  looks ahead, from one step to the whole episode, with each family's ideas at the top of its column.
- **The tree shows every station.** An algorithm built on one from another line (DQN from Q-learning, DDPG from DQN)
  hangs from a dotted marker of it, which leads to it.
- **Cards write their formulas as a sheet**: a line of words beside each formula, one formula a row, in columns that
  end level.

## Better layout

- **Formulas fit their room.** They grow a little where a card has space, and on a phone they break at their signs
  instead of scrolling sideways. An equation number never touches its formula.
- **No line starts with punctuation or a sign**, in the text, the demos and the Lab. Inline formulas break only after
  a sign, never inside p(s′, r | s, a).
- **Story formulas show whole or not at all.** The faint previews under the pictures are gone: a formula opens when a
  step uses it and closes when none does. The picture keeps one size through a story, and the stage stays inside the
  window, formula included, from 1024×768 up. On a phone the stage leaves room for the text, and a training chart
  takes the place of a test episode's drawings.
- **The Lab's "This step" breaks long lines of numbers** into one line per move instead of scrolling them; names,
  notes and status lines fit their panes, and long names show in full on hover.

## Good to know

- These changes have been checked in headless Chromium at 1440, 1366, 1280 and 1024 pixels wide and at phone size,
  on every story, textbook, card, appendix page and Lab experiment, but not yet in the real browsers that checked
  1.0.2 and 1.0.3.

Everything else is as in [1.1.0](https://github.com/DrippingSoup22/rl_atlas/releases/tag/v1.1.0).
